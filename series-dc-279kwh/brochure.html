Series DC — 279 kWh DC Battery Cabinet

The Coulomb Technology Series DC is a high-capacity, modular DC battery cabinet engineered for commercial, industrial, and utility-scale energy storage deployments. Built on our proprietary Sodium-Ion NFPP chemistry, the Series DC delivers exceptional cycle life, zero thermal runaway risk, and a 20-year design life — all in a compact, rack-integrated form factor.

Sodium-Ion NFPP vs. Lead-Acid

The Series DC's proprietary NFPP (Na-Fe-Mn-P-O) sodium-ion chemistry delivers a step-change improvement over legacy lead-acid technology across every key performance metric — while eliminating toxic materials and dramatically reducing total cost of ownership.

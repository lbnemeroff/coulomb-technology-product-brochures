Product Overview
12V Sodium-Ion Battery Pack

12V 30 / 60 / 100Ah Sodium-Ion Battery Pack

The ultimate 12V battery solution for RV, marine, off-grid, and mobility applications. Available in 30Ah, 60Ah, and 100Ah capacities to match your power needs. Sodium-ion chemistry delivers superior safety, extreme temperature performance, and maintenance-free operation — making it the ideal drop-in replacement for lead-acid batteries.

Complete battery system includes:
Sodium-ion prismatic cells (4S configuration)
Integrated BMS with protective circuitry
Rugged weatherproof enclosure
Standard 12V terminals with protective covers
State-of-charge LED indicator
Over-voltage & short-circuit protection
Key Benefits
Safety-First Design
Sodium-ion chemistry eliminates fire risks associated with lithium-ion batteries — enabling safe use in enclosed spaces.
  • Zero thermal runaway risk
  • Non-hazardous air shipping
  • No toxic gas emissions
  • Safe for enclosed compartments
Superior Performance
Outperforms both lithium-ion and lead-acid in demanding conditions — from arctic cold to desert heat.
  • 3,000+ cycles at 80% DoD
  • −40°C to +60°C operation
  • 40% lighter than lead-acid
  • Fast charging capability
Maintenance-Free
True drop-in replacement requiring no special equipment or ongoing maintenance — just install and use.
  • No watering or equalization
  • No sulfation or memory effect
  • Works with standard 12V chargers
  • Built-in BMS protection
